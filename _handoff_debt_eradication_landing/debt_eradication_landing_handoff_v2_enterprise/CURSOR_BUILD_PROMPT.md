Implement the included `DebtEradicationLandingPage.tsx` at enterprise quality. Do not simplify the design into a generic landing page.

1. Place the page at `src/pages/DebtEradicationLandingPage.tsx`.
2. Add a route for `/debt-eradication-guide`.
3. Keep the page sections exactly in this order:
   - Header trust strip with no logo
   - Hero with headline, trust mini-cards, lead form, and guide mockup
   - Exclusive video section
   - What you will discover inside
   - Stats strip
   - Testimonials
   - Bottom CTA

4. Keep the dark navy / gold cinematic look.
5. Keep the gradients, glow, shadows, border highlights, and premium CTA button.
6. Do not add a logo unless an approved asset is supplied.
7. If the final logo-free guide PNG is supplied, set `GUIDE_MOCKUP_SRC`.
8. If video thumbnail/video link is supplied, set `VIDEO_THUMBNAIL_SRC` and `VIDEO_EMBED_URL`.
9. Keep the lead form wired to `submitLeadCapture`.
10. Test desktop, tablet, and mobile.
