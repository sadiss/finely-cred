# Video Scene Prompt / Thumbnail Direction

Use this if you want to generate a custom thumbnail for the hero video.

## Scene

A premium credit/funding strategy video thumbnail.

## Visual direction

- 16:9 cinematic video thumbnail.
- Professional Black male credit/funding consultant seated in a refined office or boardroom.
- Laptop open on desk.
- Soft warm office lighting.
- Dark navy overlay on the left side for text.
- Text area should support:
  "Unlock Better Credit & Financial Opportunities — Faster."
- Gold play button centered slightly right.
- Professional, confident, premium, trustworthy.
- No clutter.
- No cartoon elements.
- No exaggerated luxury.
- No logos in the image itself unless placed separately in code.

## Colors

- Deep navy
- Emerald green
- Warm gold
- Ivory highlights
- Subtle shadows

## Negative prompt

Avoid: cheap stock photo, messy desk, bright corporate blue, cartoon, neon, overly casual clothing, distorted faces, weird hands, text misspellings, fake logos.
