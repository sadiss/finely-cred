# Google Labs video playbook (owner-facing)

**Spine:** [`FINELY_UNIFIED_MASTER_PLAN.md`](./FINELY_UNIFIED_MASTER_PLAN.md) · **In-app path:** Admin → **Content Studio** → **Video** (`/admin/content-studio?room=video`)

Finely does **not** replace Google Labs for generative video today. You produce outside Finely; Finely is the **control room** for import, destinations, publish checklist, and Hannah promotion.

---

## Produce in Google Labs

1. Draft script in Finely (Jordan workspace or your doc) — align to **Esther’s week focus** (lane + city + offer).  
2. Generate in **Google Labs** (or your editor of choice).  
3. **Export master:** MP4, **16:9** for long-form; keep a clean master before cropping shorts.  
4. For shorts: export or crop **9:16**, 30–60 seconds, hook in first 3 seconds.

**Tip:** One long master → one Resources lesson + YouTube upload + 1–2 Shorts clips (same message, tighter hook).

---

## Bring into Finely (five steps)

| Step | You do |
|------|--------|
| **1 Import** | Upload the Google Labs MP4 in Content Studio → Video → Import |
| **2 Understand** | Confirm title, topics, compliance line in the summary |
| **3 Destinations** | Toggle: Public Resources, tour, funnel hero, course — only what you need |
| **4 Publish** | Copy YouTube title/description/tags checklist; set public on Resources when ready |
| **5 Promote** | Copy **Hannah** link (`utm_content=video:{id}`) → post Shorts/social; check **Results** for signups |
