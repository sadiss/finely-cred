# VantageScore, industry FICO, UltraFICO

## VantageScore 3.0

- Very common on **free credit apps** and some bank dashboards.
- Helpful for **trends** — not a promise of mortgage or card underwriting score.
- Consumer-favoring line: “Your app is one model — your lender may pull another.”

## VantageScore 4.0

- Appears in some **GSE / mortgage-adjacent** paths; adoption expanding.
- Still **not** identical to FICO 8/9/10 or classic mortgage FICO 2/4/5.

## Industry scores

| Score | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **FICO Bankcard** | Credit card underwriting |
| **FICO Auto** | Auto lending |

Same person, same day — three different numbers is normal across models and bureaus.

## UltraFICO

- **Opt-in** program linking deposit-account behavior.
- Not default; not a substitute for on-time payments and utilization coaching.
- No Finely promise that UltraFICO will “fix” a file.

## Why scores differ (teach this)

1. Different **statistical models** weight factors differently.
2. Different **bureaus** hold different tradelines and inquiries.
3. Different **purposes** (card vs auto vs mortgage) pull different industry scores.
4. **Timing** — balances and inquiries change daily.
