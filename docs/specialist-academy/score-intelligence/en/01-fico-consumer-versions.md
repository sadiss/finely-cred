# FICO 8, 9, 10, and 10T

Educational only — adoption **varies** by creditor and product.

## FICO 8

- Common baseline for **credit cards** and many consumer lenders.
- Partners may see FICO 8 on some bureau pulls or lender disclosures.
- Still not universal — always ask what was pulled for **this** application.

## FICO 9

- Generally treats **paid collections** differently than FICO 8 in some cases.
- **Medical collections** may weigh differently — lender adoption is mixed.
- Do not promise “FICO 9 will jump X points” after a single action.

## FICO 10 and FICO 10T

- **FICO 10** — newer general-generation model.
- **FICO 10T** — adds **trended** data (how balances change over time).
- Rollout is **not** complete across all card and bank portfolios — treat as “ask the lender.”

## Coaching script

> “Which bureau, which FICO generation, and was it for a card, auto, or mortgage file? Soft pull or hard?”

## Never teach

- **“FICO 5.8”** as a real product (common 5 + 8 mashup).
- That one app score equals what underwriting will use.
