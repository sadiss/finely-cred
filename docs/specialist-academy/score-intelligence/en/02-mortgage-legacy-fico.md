# Legacy mortgage FICO (tri-merge)

Many mortgage workflows still use **older FICO generations** per bureau — not the score on a free Vantage app.

## Classic mapping (educational)

| Bureau | Common mortgage model name |
| --- | --- |
| Equifax | **Beacon 5.0** (FICO 5 family) |
| Experian | **FICO 2** (classic risk model v2) |
| TransUnion | **FICO 4** (classic 04) |

## Middle score

When three bureau scores exist, many programs use the **median** (middle) score for qualification — underwriting rules vary. Teach the concept; do not guarantee a rate from a single bureau number.

## Older versions

Specialists may hear “FICO 5” on Equifax — align language to **Beacon 5.0** when educating.

### “FICO 5.4” in the wild

Partners and forums sometimes say **“FICO 5.4.”** Finely does **not** assert that as an official FICO product name. If a client insists, document what their disclosure or LO said and teach **Beacon 5.0** on Equifax instead.

### “FICO 5.8”

Not a real model — common confusion blending **FICO 5** (mortgage) with **FICO 8** (cards).

## Who uses what

- **Mortgage originators** — tri-merge with classic FICO generations (above).
- **Cards / unsecured** — often FICO 8 or 9 (varies).
- **Auto** — FICO Auto industry score (see lesson 03).
