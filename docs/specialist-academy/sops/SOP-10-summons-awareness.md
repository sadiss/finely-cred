# SOP-10 — Summons awareness

**Purpose:** Protect clients from missed court deadlines; keep Finely files litigation-ready.

---

## Triggers

- Client uploads or mentions summons, complaint, citation, or court date.
- Partner Debt Center scenario `summons_served` or `post_35_days`.
- Any legal document in Vault tagged debt/summons.

---

## Immediate actions (same business day)

1. **Upload** complete PDF/scan to Documents Vault.
2. **Extract dates**
   - Date served (if stated).
   - Answer deadline (often 20–35 days — **read the document**).
   - Court name, case number, plaintiff.
3. **Create tasks** with reminders at T-7, T-3, T-1 days before deadline.
4. **Pause** trainee-initiated mass bureau campaigns until lead review unless counsel clears.
5. **Script to client (educational)**
   - Do not ignore mail from the court.
   - Finely does not provide legal representation; consider licensed counsel.
6. **Product letter support**
   - Outline types: `summons_response_affidavit`, `affidavit_of_dispute` — for draft/education under supervision, not unsupervised filing advice.

---

## Affidavits awareness

- Some jurisdictions require **sworn** responses or affidavits supporting defenses.
- Trainee role: ensure documents are in Vault, deadlines on task board, handoff notes for counsel.
- Trainee does **not** invent defenses or tell client to perjure.

---

## Escalate to counsel when

- Answer deadline within 14 days and no counsel retained.
- Default judgment language in mail.
- Client is confused about service validity.
- Amount or cross-claims exceed trainee scope (tenant policy).

---

## Documentation minimum

- [ ] Summons PDF in Vault
- [ ] Case number in Debt Center
- [ ] Deadline tasks assigned (admin + partner as needed)
- [ ] Note: “Court track active — bureau round X deferred until [date/lead clearance]”
