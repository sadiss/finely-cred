# SOP-14 — Prior-company / mid-round intake

**Purpose:** Never blindly restart Round 1 when another vendor or the client already started the sequence.

---

## Intake questions (required on every new file)

1. Have you worked with another credit company? Name and dates?
2. What letters were sent — bureau, collector, or both? Approximate months?
3. Any validation or cease-and-desist already mailed?
4. Any summons or court papers?
5. Current round self-report (0–3+) — verify against uploads, do not trust alone.
6. What results so far (deletions, verifications, no response)?

---

## Document review

- Ask for PDFs or photos of prior letters and certified receipts.
- Upload all to Vault; tag with send date if known.
- Map to accounts still on current report.

---

## Decision matrix

| Finding | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Prior Round 1 only, no responses yet | Finely may continue Round 1 **only** if letters were deficient — lead approval; vary OCR |
| Prior Round 2 sent | Start at Round 2 follow-up or Round 3 — **not** new Round 1 boilerplate |
| Duplicate validation in window | Do not resend identical validation — assess response status |
| Cookie-cutter templates from prior vendor | Rewrite structure; document prior sends to avoid contradiction |
| Unknown history | Conservative: FOIA-style inventory from client + one coached Round with exhibits |

---

## Example — Credit Pro Round 2 on file

Partner provides March PDFs: Round 2 to EQ/EX for Chase charge-off. Finely logs sends, continues **follow-up Round 2** with new OCR structure—does not reset Round 1.
