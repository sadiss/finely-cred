# SOP-12 — Complaints ladder

**Purpose:** Escalate to regulators or AG channels with discipline — after restore rounds fail when warranted.

---

## Preconditions (all required)

1. SOP-11 Round 1 and Round 2 completed **or** documented reason Round 2 skipped (e.g. bureau no-response).
2. Vault contains: letters, mailing proof, bureau responses, exhibits.
3. Narrative is **single coherent story** — dates, accounts, harm (inaccurate reporting), requested remedy.
4. Lead approval per tenant policy (if required).

**Typical timing:** Consider after Round 2 failure, not day after Round 1.

---

## Ladder (use highest appropriate step)

| Step | Channel | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Furnisher direct | Bureau directed you to furnisher; no resolution |
| 2 | **CFPB** | Pattern of non-investigation or clearly unsupported verification |
| 3 | **State AG** | Consumer protection angle; collector/bureau in state |
| 4 | **BBB** | Optional transparency; not a substitute for 2–3 |
| 5 | Counsel referral | Harm quantified; litigation posture |

---

## Steps

1. Draft complaint facts from **file evidence only**.
2. Attach same exhibits consumers would expect regulators to review (PDFs).
3. One complaint per logical issue chain — no duplicate spam across portals same week.
4. Log complaint ID and date in tasks.
5. Continue Round 3 only if playbook supports parallel track — avoid contradictory asks.

---

## Anti-patterns

- Complaint before any mail sent.
- Emotional language, threats, or unrelated grievances.
- Copy-paste complaints across clients (OCR/regulator pattern risk).

---

## Product note

Dashboard/compliance tracking may reference CFPB-style pathways — use as **tracker**, not automation of filing without human review.
