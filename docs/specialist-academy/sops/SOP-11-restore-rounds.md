# SOP-11 — Restore rounds (bureau file)

**Purpose:** Execute FCRA-oriented factual dispute rounds with mail discipline and round-stage awareness.

**Prerequisite:** SOP-08 clearance (debt/summons gates); SOP-13 evidence ready; SOP-14 intake complete.

---

## Round definitions

### Round 1 — Investigation request

- Target highest-severity negatives first (Credit Intel severity / charge-off / active collection reporting).
- Attach exhibit: screenshot of **that** account on report.
- Request investigation; method of verification where playbook suggests (`negativePlaybooks.ts`).
- Export PDF; certified mail recommended; log tracking.

### Round 2 — Response-driven

- Upload bureau results (30-day window typical — track actual dates).
- Quote furnisher/bureau response; identify contradictions vs exhibit.
- Tighten factual asks; no new invented disputes.

### Round 3+ — Supported escalation

- Only when R1/R2 documented and unresolved items remain with factual basis.
- Consider direct furnisher dispute if bureau loop failed.
- Pre-complaint review if still stuck (SOP-12).

---

## Per-round checklist

- [ ] Round number explicit in letter footer or cover sheet
- [ ] Account list matches exhibits (no orphan screenshots)
- [ ] OCR variation from prior round (SOP-13)
- [ ] Tasks updated: `review_results`, `follow_up`, `upload_document` from playbooks
- [ ] Partner sees status in portal (no score promises)

---

## Do not

- Restart Round 1 templates when SOP-14 shows Round 2 already sent.
- Dispute accurate information client admitted is theirs.
- Batch identical letters to all three bureaus without per-bureau exhibit when reporting differs.
