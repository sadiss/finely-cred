# F03 — §809 framing

**Front:** What may we say about FDCPA §809 in client education?

**Back:** Consumer may **request validation** within the window described on the collector notice (often taught as 30 days from first written communication). Collector must **cease collection until validation** as the statute provides — educational summary, not a promise of deletion or lawsuit outcome.
