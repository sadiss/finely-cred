# FICO-01 — Score talk-track (never sell a naked number)

## Flash

**Front:** Partner asks “What’s my score?” What do you say first?

**Back:** “Which **model**, which **bureau**, for what **purpose**, and was it **soft or hard**? Educational only — we don’t guarantee approvals.”

---

## Teach this like a lesson

### Plain explanation

A three-digit number without context is marketing, not literacy. Finely teaches **model literacy** so partners stop comparing app Vantage scores to mortgage Beacon 5.0.

### Models to name accurately

- FICO 8 / 9 / 10 / 10T (adoption varies)
- Mortgage classics: Beacon 5.0 (EQ), FICO 2 (EX), FICO 4 (TU) — middle score often used
- Vantage 3.0 (apps) vs 4.0 (some GSE paths)
- FICO Auto / Bankcard industry scores
- UltraFICO = opt-in, not default

### Never teach

- **“FICO 5.8”** as a real model (5+8 mashup)
- **“FICO 5.4”** as official — use **Beacon 5.0** on Equifax

### Haitian desk

Explain in Kreyòl *why* the app number differs from the bank pull; keep bureau letter language in English.

### Compliance

Educational only. Not legal advice. No guaranteed loan, card, or score outcomes.
