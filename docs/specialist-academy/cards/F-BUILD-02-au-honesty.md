# F-BUILD-02 — AU honesty

**Front:** Client asks if authorized-user accounts will “fix everything.” What do you say?

**Back:** A few AU trades **may** help history/mix **sometimes** — impact is **limited and conditional**; lenders differ; **no** promised score jumps, card approvals, or lender acceptance. Never coach hiding the primary account holder relationship.

Use the flash answer as the headline, then coach the **why**, **timeline**, and **portal clicks** — never promise deletions, scores, or court wins.

2. Match the card topic to the correct SOP (see WORKFLOW-MAP).

4. Log sends in the portal; screenshot evidence the same day.
5. Debrief in Kreyòl when needed; store English letter text.
