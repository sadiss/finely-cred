# SCI-02 — Why scores differ by product

**Front:** Partner says Credit Karma disagrees with the mortgage LO. One sentence?

**Back:** Different **model**, **bureau**, **purpose**, and **pull date** — ask what the lender used for **this** loan.

---

## Teach

- App Vantage 3.0 ≠ mortgage Beacon 5.0 / FICO 2 / FICO 4 middle score.
- Card bank may use FICO Bankcard; dealer may use FICO Auto.
- Finely restores **report accuracy** — does not control which model a lender selects.
