# F01 — Debt-first

**Front:** Client has active collection tradeline and a collector letter dated last week. What runs before a big Round 1 bureau campaign?

**Back:** **Debt-first:** triage (SOP-08), validation on collector debt (SOP-09), document in Debt Center. Bureau restore on that account is gated until the collector track is started and any summons risk is cleared (SOP-10).
