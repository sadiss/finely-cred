# SCI-03 — Consumer-first coaching (no guarantees)

**Front:** Partner asks “What score do I need for funding?”

**Back:** “Funding is Nora/lender territory — we document restore readiness. For **your** number, which model and bureau did you see, and for what product?”

---

## Teach

- Consumer-favoring: explain literacy so they stop chasing the wrong number.
- Never promise a threshold (720, 740, etc.) without model context.
- Hand off stacking to **Nora** separately — no cross-brand spam in Finely comms.
