# Tracks A–E — Operations layers (full guide)

Track **F** teaches restore methodology. Tracks **A–E** are the **professional habits** that make Track F work in the portal: consent, communication, language access, evidence, and letters. Specialists should complete all layers—not only dispute templates.

---

## Track A — Onboarding, consent, portal hygiene

### Plain explanation

Before disputes, the partner must be **properly onboarded**: agreement on file, portal access working, documents uploading to the Vault—not scattered in text messages.

### Why it matters

Without consent and upload discipline, letters lack exhibits, audit trails break, and regulators see chaos. Wealth paths depend on **clean files**.

### Step-by-step

1. Confirm program / agreement in billing or entitlements (tenant process).
2. Walk partner through **one successful report upload** (HTML preferred; PDF re-parse if needed).
3. Show Documents Vault paths: report, IDs, collector mail, summons.
4. Set communication norms: portal messages for case updates; no SSN in chat.

### Example — new partner “Claudette”

Claudette signs restore program Friday. Specialist schedules 20-minute portal tour Monday: upload IdentityIQ HTML, confirm Collections tab loads, create first task “Mail validation—Summit Receivables.” Consent checkbox captured in tenant workflow.

> **Mistake:** Starting letters before partner can log in or find uploaded PDFs.

### Checklist

- [ ] Agreement active
- [ ] Portal login verified
- [ ] Vault folder structure explained
- [ ] Consent captured per tenant SOP

## Track B — Partner communication & expectations

### Plain explanation

Finely voice is **warm, credible, restore-for-wealth**—not hype. Partners hear **process**, **timelines**, and **honest limits**.

### Why it matters

Over-promising creates refunds, complaints, and ethical risk. Under-communicating creates “they ghosted me” reviews.

### Step-by-step

1. Open with what happens **this week** (validation, evidence, Round 1 prep).
2. State what Finely **does not** guarantee (deletions, scores, Nora approval).
3. End with **one clear next action** for partner (upload X, sign Y, attend call).

### Example message (English)

“Hi James—this week we’re mailing validation to the collector on your March letter and saving your Experian exhibit for the bureau round. Timelines vary by bureau mail; we’ll update tasks when responses arrive. This is educational restore work, not a promise to delete every item.”

### Checklist

- [ ] No score/funding guarantees
- [ ] Next action dated in tasks
- [ ] Tone: professional, calm

## Track C — Haitian desk (Kreyòl-first)

### Plain explanation

Many partners think best in **Kreyòl**. Specialists explain strategy sit-together; **bureau and collector letters remain English** unless a bilingual template exists.

### Why it matters

Trust and comprehension reduce missed deadlines. English letters match furnisher expectations.

### Example — validation sit-together

Specialist reads key English phrases on validation letter while explaining in Kreyòl: “Lèt sa mande prèv dèt la. Nou voye li certified pou gen dat.” Partner repeats mailing date aloud.

### Checklist

- [ ] Kreyòl explanation documented in notes
- [ ] Letters English unless template says otherwise
- [ ] Calendar dates emphasized

## Track D — Evidence, reports, Credit Intel

### Plain explanation

Disputes without exhibits are weak. Credit Intel timeline: **evidence → Round 1 → mail → follow-up**.

### Example

Charge-off on EX: screenshot tradeline with balance and status; attach as Exhibit A; dispute only visible fields.

See SOP-13 and `docs/COLLECTIONS_AUDIT.md` for matching collection evidence keys.

### Checklist

- [ ] Report pull date recorded
- [ ] Per-account screenshots
- [ ] Credit Intel “next” step aligned

---

## Track E — Letters, templates, OCR-safe variation

### Plain explanation

Admin Templates generate **variants**. Specialists vary structure so letters are not OCR-detected bulk spam.

### Example

Round 1 account A: facts → request → exhibit list. Account B: request → facts → MOFV clause. Same truth, different layout.

### Checklist

- [ ] Variant or manual reorder used
- [ ] Certified mail habit
- [ ] PDF saved to Vault
