# Metro 2 factual disputes (field discipline)

## Learning objectives

- Read tradelines as **Metro 2 data segments** (conceptual), not story paragraphs.
- Craft **factual** disputes tied to one or two fields per round.
- Avoid cookie-cutter bulk letters (SOP-13 OCR discipline).

## Field families that matter

- **Account status / payment rating** — current vs delinquent coding errors.
- **Date of first delinquency (DOFD)** — drives obsolescence math (educational).
- **Balance & high credit** — inconsistencies across bureaus.
- **Compliance condition codes** — disputed by consumer flags.

## Round architecture

### Round 1

- Claim: specific inaccuracy + exhibit (statement snippet, correspondence).
- Ask: reinvestigate and correct or delete if unverifiable.

### Round 2

- Input: bureau/furnisher response letter.
- Claim: contradiction (“verified” but prior letter shows different balance).
- Exhibit: side-by-side bureau comparisons from Vault.

### Round 3+

- Only with new artifacts — CFPB narrative when SOP-12 criteria met.

## e-OSCAR literacy (no vendor worship)

- Furnishers respond through industry pipes; consumer sees outcomes in mail.
- Specialist tracks **what changed** on each bureau pull — not rumor from social media.

## Common failure modes

- Disputing “I don’t like this account” without factual hook.
- Mailing 40-page packets without index → weak partner experience.
- Promising deletion because “Metro 2 requires” — overclaim.

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] Field named in letter matches pull screenshot
- [ ] One bureau at a time when learning; tri-merge when partner ready
- [ ] Response PDF uploaded before next round drafted
