# Restore rounds — Round 1 vs Round 2

Bureau restore at Finely means **FCRA-oriented factual disputes**: you show what the report says, what is wrong or unverified, and what you want investigated. Rounds are **not** the same letter with a new date.

## Why it matters

Round 1 opens the investigation. Round 2 **uses bureau answers**—quoting their verification language, highlighting contradictions with your exhibit. Skipping straight to “Round 3 delete” without Round 2 discipline triggers complaints too early and looks emotional to regulators.

## Round comparison

| Round | Purpose | Must include |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Round 1** | Request investigation; establish facts | Account screenshot exhibit; specific fields disputed |
| **Round 2** | Respond to bureau/furnisher results | Copy of bureau response; tightened MOFV asks |
| **Round 3+** | Supported persistence | Lead review; no template spam |

## Step-by-step Round 1

1. SOP-13 evidence on **that** account.
2. Pick playbook clause for negative type (`negativePlaybooks.ts`).
3. Vary template structure from last client send.
4. Export PDF; certified mail; save tracking.
5. Task: `review_results` with expected response window.

## Step-by-step Round 2

1. Upload bureau mail/results to Vault.
2. Quote their verification statement beside your exhibit.
3. Ask for method of verification and source records.
4. Change letter layout (OCR SOP)—not copy-paste Round 1.

## Example — collection tradeline

**Round 1:** EX collections row shows balance $920, DOFD 2019-04; exhibit screenshot `collections_tradeline_0`. Letter requests investigation of accuracy and verification method.

**Bureau responds:** “Verified as reported.”

**Round 2:** Letter attaches exhibit again, quotes response, notes missing itemization vs collector letter partner holds, requests MOFV documentation. **Different opening paragraph** than Round 1.

> **Example — mistake:** Mailing Round 1 again with only a new date because “Round 2 template is boring.”

## Example — Haitian desk partner

Explain in Kreyòl: “Premye lèt mande yo verifye. Dezyèm lèt montre repons yo epi mande prèv.” Letter remains English.

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] Round number on cover sheet
- [ ] Exhibits match account list
- [ ] Prior round PDFs referenced in Round 2+
- [ ] Complaints not started before Round 2 unless documented exception

## Compliance reminder

> **Compliance:** Dispute accurate information the consumer admits is theirs is prohibited coaching. Educational process only.
