# Evidence, OCR discipline & complaints ladder

Finely letters should look **human-prepared**, and Finely exhibits should prove **what the report actually shows**. Complaints are a **late-stage tool**, not a first punch.

## Screenshot evidence (SOP-13)

**Good exhibit:** Full tradeline or collection card visible—creditor name, status, balance, DOFD if disputed—plus enough bureau context to orient a reviewer.

**Weak exhibit:** Cropped FICO gauge with no account detail.

### Example

Partner disputes EX collection “ABC Recovery.” Specialist saves evidence with `sectionKey` `collections_tradeline`, `creditorName` matching dispute candidate. Letter cites “Exhibit A — Experian collection tradeline dated [report pull].”

> **Mistake:** Screenshot from a different bureau than the letter recipient without explanation.

## OCR / non-repetitive templates

Rotate section order, swap playbook clauses, use Admin Templates variant versions. Ten accounts should not produce ten byte-identical bodies.

### Example

Account A opens with factual summary; Account B opens with investigation request then facts—same truth, different structure.

## Complaints ladder (SOP-12)

**When appropriate:** Round 1 and 2 documented, unresolved inaccuracy, coherent narrative, exhibits attached.

**When inappropriate:** Day after Round 1; emotional duplicate CFPB filings; facts contradict validation letter.

### Example

After Round 2, furnisher never supplied MOFV for charged-off auto tradeline. Specialist drafts **one** CFPB narrative with dates, account labels, PDF exhibits—logs complaint ID in tasks.

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] Per-account exhibits
- [ ] Template variation logged
- [ ] Complaint pre-check with lead if required
- [ ] No revenge spam language

## Compliance reminder
