# Prior company & round-stage intake

Partners often arrive mid-flight. Finely’s credibility comes from **reading the timeline**, not resetting to marketing “Round 1” because our template is prettier.

## Why it matters

Duplicate Round 1 sends confuse furnishers, waste certified mail, and embarrass partners who already have proof of prior disputes.

## Step-by-step (SOP-14)

1. Ask scripted intake questions (vendor name, months active, letters sent, validation/summons, self-reported round).
2. Request PDFs of prior letters and receipts.
3. Map sends to accounts still on current report.
4. Decide: continue Round 2+, follow-up validation, or coached Round 1 only with lead approval.
5. Portal note template with round and next SOP.

## Example — “They did Round 2 last month”

Upload prior PDFs showing Round 2 to Experian for Chase charge-off. Finely starts **Round 2 follow-up** citing bureau response partner uploaded—or Round 3 if facts support. OCR rewrite required.

> **Mistake:** Blanket new Round 1 to all three bureaus.

## Example — cookie-cutter prior vendor

Prior letters are identical paragraphs across eight accounts. Finely documents prior sends, **restructures** language, does not contradict dates/facts partner already mailed.

## Haitian desk

Conduct intake sit-together in Kreyòl; type notes in English for letter consistency.

- [ ] Prior vendor named

## Compliance reminder

> **Compliance:** Never fabricate history. Educational process only.
