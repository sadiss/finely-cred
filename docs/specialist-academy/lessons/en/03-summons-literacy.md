# Summons literacy

A summons is not “another letter.” It is a **calendar event**. Finely specialists are not counsel, but we **must** protect partners from missed deadlines and keep Vault records clean for counsel if retained.

## Why it matters

Answer windows are often **20–35 days** depending on jurisdiction and document type. Ignoring court mail can lead to default judgments—harm that no bureau dispute fixes.

## Step-by-step (SOP-10)

1. Same business day: upload **complete** summons/complaint PDF to Vault.
2. Extract plaintiff, case number, date served, **answer due date** (read the paper—do not guess).
3. Create tasks at T-7, T-3, T-1 before deadline.
4. Pause trainee-driven **mass bureau campaigns** until lead review.
5. Use educational script: do not ignore court; consider licensed attorney.
6. Product outlines (`summons_response_affidavit`) are for supervised drafting—not unsupervised filing advice.

## Example — served at work

**Facts:** Partner uploads scan: *Capital One vs. Partner*, served April 2, answer due May 7 (state civil rule cited on page 2).

**Actions:**

- Case card in Debt Center with May 7 deadline.
- Admin task assigned; partner message with plain English calendar summary.
- **No** Round 1 batch that night.
- Note in file: “Court track active—bureau round deferred per SOP-10.”

> **Example — Haitian desk:** In Kreyòl, stress **dat tribinal** louder than score talk. Show where the date appears on the PDF.

## Example — partner wants to “dispute it away”

**Facts:** Partner believes the debt is wrong and refuses to “answer” because “Finely will delete it.”

**Actions:** Explain distinction—**bureau accuracy work** does not replace **court procedure**. Encourage counsel consult. Continue documenting report inaccuracies separately without telling partner to ignore the summons.

> **Mistake — red flag:** Any script that says “ignore the court, we got this.”

## Affidavits awareness

Some responses require **sworn** statements. Trainee role: ensure documents are in Vault, deadlines on the board, handoff packet for counsel—not invent defenses.

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] Lead notified if &lt; 14 days remain
- [ ] Bureau campaign pause documented
- [ ] Partner script: educational, not representation
