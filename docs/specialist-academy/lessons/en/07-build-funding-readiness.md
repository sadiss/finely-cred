# BUILD & funding-readiness

**Restore** corrects the file; **BUILD** teaches how a consumer profile often looks stronger through an **underwriting lens**—while debt gates and disputes still run. Finely is not the lender. Nora is a **separate** pathway when configured.

## Why it matters

Partners join Finely for **wealth paths**. Specialists who only talk deletions miss the chance to coach payment discipline, utilization, and mix—without promising cards or approvals.

## Teaching targets (industry education—not lender law)

| Element | Typical coaching target |
| --- | --- |
| Revolving | **2+** cards/trades in good standing |
| Mix | Not only one product type |
| Installment | **1** healthy installment reporting |
| AU | May help **sometimes**—limited, conditional; lender rules vary |

**Aspirational framing:** Premium card / “black card” liquidity talk is allowed only as **labeled aspiration**—not a Finely product promise.

## FICO-style factors (educational weights)

| Factor | Common teaching share |
| --- | --- |
| Payment history | ~35% |
| Amounts owed / utilization | ~30% |
| Length of history | ~15% |
| New credit / inquiries | ~10% |
| Credit mix | ~10% |

Lenders also weigh income, DTI, reserves—Finely does not control those.

## Example — BUILD during Round 1 wait

Partner has two maxed cards and a car loan paid on time. While bureau Round 1 is in mail transit, coach: pay down revolving before new applications; explain utilization in plain language; **do not** promise 80-point jump.

## Example — AU honesty

Partner’s aunt offers AU on a 20-year Amex. Script: “Authorized user history **may** help some models and lenders; it is not guaranteed; some lenders ignore AU; we never lie about who owns the account.”

> **Mistake:** “Add three AUs and Nora approves you.”

## Example — installment

Partner only has debit-style history. Discuss secured card + existing auto installment reporting as **mix education**—not “open this loan today” during active summons.

## SOP-15 before Nora

Complete funding-readiness checklist: file hygiene + BUILD notes + script that Finely ≠ lender.
