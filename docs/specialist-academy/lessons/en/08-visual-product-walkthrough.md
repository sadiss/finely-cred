# Visual walkthrough — restore → mail (demo data)

This lesson is **self-guided**: use the interactive gallery below the prose to click through each portal step. Every frame is **SAMPLE / DEMO** — no real SSN, DOB, or full account numbers.

## Why specialists learn the clicks

Partners call you while staring at their phone. If you cannot say “tap Credit Restore → upload report → dispute this tradeline → attach screenshot → mail,” they lose trust. Consumer power is **knowing the process** and **documenting** each step.

## The chain (seven beats + debt fork)

1. **Portal** — Credit Restore entry from the partner dashboard.
2. **Credit Intel** — upload or open parsed tri-merge; negatives become dispute candidates.
3. **Tradeline detail** — screenshot exhibits on the **actual** reported fields (demo •••• 4821).
4. **Letter builder** — factual FCRA disputes; vary templates (OCR-safe).
5. **Evidence gate** — compliance blocks send until exhibits attach.
6. **Mail** — preview PDF, certified mail / LetterStream, log tracking.
7. **Tasks** — Round 2 windows, validation follow-ups, complaint timing.

When **collection or summons risk** exists, branch to **Debt Center** (validation first) and **Litigation Command** (calendar + counsel) before heavy bureau campaigning.

## Consumer power reminder

You are teaching **rights exercised on paper** — validation, investigation requests, exhibits — not illegal shortcuts or guaranteed deletions.
