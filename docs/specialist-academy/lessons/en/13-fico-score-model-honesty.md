# FICO & score model honesty

## Learning objectives

- Separate **educational scores** (Vantage, app scores) from **mortgage tri-merge FICO** versions.
- Explain **FICO 10/10T** adoption without fear marketing.
- Coach partners on **soft vs hard** inquiries and utilization literacy.

## Model stack (simplified)

| Context | Typical model | Partner message |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Credit card app | FICO 8/9 bankcard | “Different from mortgage score.” |
| Auto | FICO Auto variants | “Purpose-specific.” |
| Mortgage | FICO 2/4/5 legacy + 10T migration | “Lender selects — ask LO.” |
| “Free” apps | Vantage 3/4 | “Directional only.” |

## FICO 10T honesty

- **Trended data** can influence risk view — not a consumer lever to “game” in 30 days.
- Teach **payment stability** and **balance trends** instead of gimmicks.
- UltraFICO/opt-in: voluntary, limited use cases — see Finely one-sheet `ultrafico-opt-in-honesty.html`.

## Utilization & AU tradelines

- Utilization affects scores; AU lines carry ethical/disclosure obligations.
- Finely positions tradelines as **education + inventory transparency** — no score guarantees.

## Partner scripts

> “What score did you see, on which bureau, for which loan type? If we don’t know, we can’t compare to underwriting.”

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] Score literacy footer pasted on partner PDFs when required
- [ ] No public posts promising “+100 points”
- [ ] BUILD/funding readiness separated from restore rounds
