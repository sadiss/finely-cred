# FCRA investigation doctrine (specialist depth)

## Learning objectives

- Map **15 U.S.C. § 1681i** responsibilities to bureau/furnisher behavior (educational framing).
- Distinguish **reinvestigation** from consumer wishes or score outcomes.
- Build **Vault-grade** exhibit chains without over-promising deletions.

## Core statute map (consumer-power lens)

| Actor | Duty (plain language) | Specialist action |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------|
| CRA (bureau) | Reasonable reinvestigation of disputed info | Log dispute receipt, track statutory clock, preserve mail trail |
| Furnisher | Duty to investigate after indirect dispute | Tie Metro 2 fields to evidence; note contradictions |
| Consumer | Right to dispute inaccurate/incomplete info | Intake: what is verifiable vs aspirational |

## Investigation timeline literacy

1. **Day 0** — Certified/tracked dispute mailed or uploaded with narrow claims.
2. **Day 5–30** — Bureau acknowledgment; furnisher ping via e-OSCAR (conceptual).
3. **Day 30+** — Result letter; classify: verified, updated, deleted, or “remains.”
4. **Round 2** — Only with **new** factual contradiction or exhibit — not rage re-mail.

## Metro 2 ↔ FCRA bridge

- Field-level accuracy beats narrative volume.
- Date of first delinquency, payment status, and balance fields must align across bureau copies.
- If furnisher verifies without supporting docs in Vault, document for escalation path (CFPB/SOP-12) — not same-day lawsuit talk.

## Honesty guardrails

- No guaranteed deletions or point gains.
- No “609 loophole” marketing — teach **permissible purpose** and **accuracy** instead.
- Counsel referral when litigation or summons is active.

## Specialist checklist

- [ ] One claim per tradeline per round where possible
- [ ] Exhibit index in Vault (ID, date, source)
- [ ] Partner told what **can** change vs what may remain
- [ ] Next round gated on bureau response, not calendar spam
