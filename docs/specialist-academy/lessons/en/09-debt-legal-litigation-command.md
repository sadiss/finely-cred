# Debt & Legal — summons, debt buyers & Litigation Command

This module matches **Debt & Summons Center** and **Litigation readiness** signals in Credit Intel—same depth as restore rounds, **consumer-power** voice.

## Why it matters

Collection lawsuits and summons are **time events**. Consumers who understand deadlines and documentation can push back **lawfully** with validation, answers (through counsel), and accurate credit disputes—Finely organizes the file; **does not** replace a lawyer.

## Rights & actions (lead with these)

- **Request validation** in writing when a collector first contacts (§809 educational).
- **Dispute inaccurate reporting** to bureaus with exhibits while debt track runs.
- **Never ignore court mail**—calendar answer dates immediately.
- **Upload everything** to Vault: collector letters, summons, payment histories, modification letters.
- **Use Debt Center letter types**: validation, cease contact (with lead review), debt dispute, summons response **outlines**, affidavits of dispute under supervision.
- **Escalate to counsel** when answer deadlines are short, amounts are large, or partner is confused about service.

## Debt buyer suits (educational)

Plaintiffs often must show **assignment** and account documentation. Consumers may:

- Demand validation before suit pressure (if pre-suit collection).
- Answer or respond through **attorney** when served—affidavits may be required in some courts.
- Continue **FCRA** disputes on reporting that does not match court or servicer records.

Finely **does not** promise “debt buyers always lose.”

## Step-by-step — summons intake

1. Same-day Vault upload.
2. Extract case #, plaintiff, served date, **answer due**.
3. Tasks T-7, T-3, T-1; notify lead.
4. Pause mass bureau campaigns until reviewed.
5. Partner script: educational urgency + counsel referral.

## Example — 35-day answer state

Summons shows 35 days from service. Specialist sets deadline tasks, opens Debt Center case, notes “court track active.” Round 1 bureau mail waits—**consumer protected from missed court date**.

## Example — affidavit awareness

Court requires sworn denial of debt. Trainee ensures partner has **attorney** to draft/file; Finely supplies organized exhibits from Vault—not DIY perjury coaching.

## Common mistakes

- “Dispute the bureau instead of answering court.”
- Promising dismissal via Finely letters alone.
- Restarting Round 1 while court clock runs.
