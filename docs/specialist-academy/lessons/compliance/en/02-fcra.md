# FCRA — accuracy, investigation & dispute rights (high level)

The **Fair Credit Reporting Act** frames how consumer reporting agencies and furnishers handle **accuracy** and **disputes**. Finely restore work lives here for **bureau rounds**—always educational, never a promise to delete accurate debts.

## Why it matters

Partners trust us when we speak precisely: “We are requesting an **investigation** of what is reported, with **evidence**.” That matches Finely’s litigation-ready file standard and differs from scam “erase everything” language.

## Core ideas (specialist level)

| Topic | Plain language |
| --- | --- |
| **Accuracy** | Reports should reflect correct account status, balances, dates, and ownership. |
| **Disputes** | Consumers may dispute incomplete or inaccurate information; bureaus generally must investigate (time frames are statutory—educational summary). |
| **Permissible purpose** | Reports are pulled for allowed reasons (credit, insurance, employment, etc.)—ties to **inquiry** playbooks. |
| **Furnisher duties** | After dispute routing, furnishers must investigate and report results—specialists track paper trail. |

Citations specialists may **name educationally**: FCRA § 611 dispute process framing; § 623 furnisher accuracy themes—**not** case-specific legal conclusions.
