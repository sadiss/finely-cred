# Metro 2 — furnisher reporting literacy

Metro 2 is the **industry data format** many furnishers use when they send account information to credit bureaus. Specialists do **not** need to be programmers—but you **do** need to know how **coding errors** show up on a consumer report so Finely disputes stay **factual** under FCRA.

**Consumer power:** When the published report shows contradictions (status vs payment grid, wrong dates), that is **your leverage**—request investigation and method of verification through the bureau dispute channel (often routed via **e-OSCAR** to the furnisher). You are not begging; you are invoking a process.

## Why it matters for restore-for-wealth

Partners often say “they’re reporting me wrong.” Sometimes the **human-readable** tradeline is inconsistent with status codes, dates, or payment history grids. Pointing to those inconsistencies—with screenshots—is stronger than repeating “delete per Metro 2” without facts.

> **Compliance:** We teach literacy. We do **not** sell “magic Metro 2 deletion codes” or guarantee outcomes.

## Plain explanation

- **Furnisher** reports data in a structured layout (Metro 2-style fields map to what you see: account status, payment rating, dates).
- **Bureaus** display a consumer-friendly version; errors can be **transcription**, **status mismatch**, or **stale** information after a modification.
- Finely disputes ask for **investigation** and **method of verification**—aligned with `negativePlaybooks.ts` clauses—not unsupported legal threats.

## Step-by-step for specialists

1. Pull report; screenshot the **tradeline** (not score-only).
2. Note fields partner disputes: status (open/closed/charge-off), DOFD, balance, payment history symbols.
3. Compare across bureaus if tri-merge—differences can be factual dispute angles.
4. Draft narrative: “Reported status X contradicts exhibit / prior bureau response.”
5. Avoid claiming “Metro 2 violation” unless your exhibit shows a specific inconsistency you can describe in plain English.

## Example — wrong status after modification

**Facts:** Partner completed a lender modification in January. Experian still shows “120 days past due” in payment grid while status line says “current.”

**Specialist approach:** Round 1 exhibit captures both lines. Letter requests investigation and MOFV; quotes visible fields. Round 2 cites bureau “verified” language if inadequate.

> **Haitian desk:** Explain in Kreyòl that we are showing **kontradiksyon sou rapò a**; letter stays English.

## Example — not magic codes

Partner saw a TikTok “use code 223.” **Do not** instruct fabricated fraud. If coding is wrong, describe what the **report shows** vs supporting mail from servicer.

> **Mistake:** Promising deletion because “Metro 2 requires it” without exhibit-backed facts.

## Common mistakes

- Disputing without tradeline screenshot.
- Identical boilerplate “Metro 2 audit” letters (OCR spam).
- Inventing account numbers or dates.
