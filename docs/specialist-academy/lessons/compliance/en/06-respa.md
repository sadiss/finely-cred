# RESPA — mortgage servicing literacy basics

The **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act** touches **mortgage servicing**, escrow, and loss-mitigation communications. When partners have **mortgage tradelines**, late payments, or **foreclosure risk**, specialists need vocabulary—not foreclosure law practice.

## Why it matters

Confusing “one late payment” with “foreclosure started” creates panic or false calm. Finely documents **reporting** and coaches partners to use **servicer written channels**; we do not file foreclosure answers.

## Educational highlights

- **Servicer notices** — borrowers often receive mailed notices before credit reporting changes; upload to Vault.
- **Escrow** — payment includes taxes/insurance components; reporting delinquency may track escrow shortages (educational).
- **Loss mitigation** — forbearance/modification programs exist; specialists do not negotiate as attorneys.

## Step-by-step

1. Identify mortgage tradeline on report; screenshot.
2. Separate **credit dispute** (FCRA accuracy) from **servicer hardship** (partner contacts servicer).
3. If foreclosure mention on report or mail → **foreclosure module** + counsel referral.
4. Factual dispute if status says “foreclosure” but partner has modification approval letter.

## Example — mortgage late vs foreclosure start

Report payment history shows 30-day late; status still “open.” Partner received no acceleration notice. Specialist: BUILD payment plan talk; Round 1 only if reporting fields wrong vs statements—not “RESPA violation” bluff.
