# Repossession — consumer education & documentation

Repossession affects **collateral** (usually a vehicle) and **credit reporting**. Finely educates partners on **notices**, **accounting**, and **credit aftermath**—and documents for counsel when legal action appears.

## Why it matters

Repo tradelines are data-heavy: sale date, deficiency balance, status codes. Product playbook (`repossession` in `negativePlaybooks.ts`) asks for **disposition and accounting**—specialists execute that with empathy.

## Educational topics

- **Notice rights** — vary by state; specialists do not give legal opinions—encourage partner to save all mail.
- **Redemption / reinstatement** — some states allow before sale—**counsel** for specifics.
- **Credit reporting** — repo may report as repossession/charge-off; disputes target **inaccurate** fields with exhibits.

1. Upload repo notice, sale letter, deficiency letter to Vault.

Sale letter shows $4,200 deficiency; report shows $9,100. Exhibit both. Round 1 factual dispute; Round 2 quotes bureau response.
