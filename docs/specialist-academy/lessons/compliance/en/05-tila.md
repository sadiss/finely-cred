# TILA — Truth in Lending literacy (funding conversations)

**TILA** requires clear disclosure of credit terms in many consumer credit transactions. Finely specialists are **not loan officers**—but partners ask about APR, payment shocks, and “what did I sign?” during **BUILD** and funding-readiness talks.

## Consumer power lens

Borrowers can **request disclosures**, compare **APR** across offers, and refuse predatory terms before signing. Teaching power means partners know they can **read and question** loan paperwork—not that Finely will rewrite their contract or guarantee funding.

## Why it matters

Restore-for-wealth includes honest money conversations. Mis-explaining TILA sounds like unauthorized lending advice. We teach **awareness**: read disclosures, compare APR, ask servicer/lender—not Finely—for contract copies.

## Educational topics

- **APR vs interest rate** — APR includes certain costs; educational comparison tool.
- **Payment schedule** — fixed vs variable awareness.
- **Right of rescission** — exists in some transactions (e.g., certain home-secured)—**escalate to counsel** for specifics.
- **Funding handoff** — Nora/lenders make credit decisions; Finely does not.

## Step-by-step for specialists

1. Stay in scope: credit **reporting** restore + BUILD habits.
2. If partner asks “is this loan illegal?” → **not our role**; suggest attorney or lender ombudsman.
3. Document that partner should retain **Truth-in-Lending disclosures** and promissory notes in Vault for their own records—not for Finely to invent terms.

## Example — new auto loan during BUILD

Partner financed a car while in Round 1 wait window. Specialist coaches: on-time payments help; keep TILA disclosure in personal files; **do not** tell them to rescind without attorney. Bureau work stays on **reporting accuracy** only.

## Example — credit card promo APR

Partner misunderstood 0% intro APR end date. Educational: read Schumer box on statement; Finely does not negotiate card contracts.

> **Mistake:** Quoting APR corrections on Finely letterhead as if we are the creditor.
