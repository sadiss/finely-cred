# FDCPA — collection practices literacy

The **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act** governs **third-party debt collectors** (not every original creditor in every situation—education level only). Sanz doctrine puts **validation (§809)** early on collector debt. This module deepens what specialists may **explain** and what requires **counsel**.

**Consumer power:** You can **push back in writing**—request validation, dispute inaccurate details, document harassment patterns, and stop contact under §805(c) when appropriate (with lead review). That is lawful strength, not “ignore your debts.”

## Why it matters

Partners receive scary calls. Specialists calm the file with **process**: validation, documented contacts, bureau accuracy work—without posing as attorneys or promising lawsuits.

## Key concepts (educational)

| Concept | Specialist framing |
| --- | --- |
| **§809 validation** | Written request for verification within the window described on collector notice (often taught as 30 days from first written communication). |
| **Harassment / abuse** | Law limits threatening, oppressive, or deceptive conduct—document facts; do not coach retaliation. |
| **Mini-Miranda** | Collectors must disclose they are attempting to collect a debt and that information obtained may be used for that purpose (wording varies—educational). |
| **Cease contact** | §805(c) pathways exist in product templates—use with lead review; not rage bait. |

## Step-by-step — validation first

1. **SOP-08** debt-first triage.
2. **SOP-09** validation certified mail.
3. Log calls: date, time, collector name, what was said (facts only).
4. If threats of suit: **SOP-10** summons check; counsel referral.

## Example — collector call (document, don’t escalate emotionally)

**Call:** Collector says “pay today or we sue tomorrow,” no prior validation response on file.

**Specialist actions:**

- Coach partner: do not admit debt inaccurately; do not give bank info on call if policy says avoid.
- Note call in file; if validation already sent, consider follow-up template `debt_validation_followup_no_response`.
- If suit threatened, intake for summons upload—not “ignore them.”
