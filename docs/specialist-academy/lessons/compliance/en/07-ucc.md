# UCC — secured transactions awareness (auto & assigned debt)

The **Uniform Commercial Code** governs many **secured transactions** (especially auto loans). Product copy in Debt Center references **burden of proof** and assignment themes—specialists stay **high-level** and **factual**.

## Consumer power lens

Consumers can **challenge who is collecting**, request **validation and assignment proof**, and dispute **inaccurate secured-loan reporting** on the bureau file. That is lawful pushback—not hiding collateral or inventing fraud.

## Why it matters

Repo and collector cases often involve “who owns the note?” Finely does not litigate UCC Article 9—but we can request **verification** and **documentation** without overclaiming.

## Educational awareness

- **Security interest** — lender may have rights in collateral (vehicle) when loan defaults (state law applies).
- **Assignment** — collectors may need to prove chain of title on debt; validation letters start that paper trail (**SOP-09**).
- **Do not** tell partners to hide vehicles or commit fraud.

## Step-by-step

1. Auto installment on report → note balance, status, lender name.
2. Repo tradeline → use `negativePlaybooks.ts` repossession tasks (disposition, accounting).
3. Validation to collector if assigned debt; bureau dispute for **inaccurate** repo dates/balances.

## Example — auto installment before repo

Partner 60 days late; no repo yet. Specialist: coach cure payments; document servicer letters; dispute only if report shows wrong delinquency dates.

## Example — assigned credit card debt

Collector demands payment. Validation requests itemization and assignment documentation—educational FDCPA/FCRA framing, not “UCC voids everything.”
