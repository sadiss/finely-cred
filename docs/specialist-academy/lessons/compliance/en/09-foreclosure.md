# Foreclosure — stages, credit impact & counsel boundaries

**Foreclosure** is a **legal process** on real property—not a single late mortgage payment. Finely specialists provide **literacy**, **documentation**, and **credit reporting disputes**—never foreclosure defense representation.

## Why it matters

Partners may face loss of housing; tone must be compassionate and precise. Wrong promises cause harm. Align with `negativePlaybooks.ts` **foreclosure** tasks: timeline verification, notices in Vault.

## Educational stages (simplified)

1. **Delinquency** — missed payments; servicer contact.
2. **Notice / breach letter** — often required before suit (state law varies).
3. **Foreclosure filing** — court or non-judicial process (state-specific).
4. **Sale / completion** — credit reporting may update to foreclosure or charge-off variants.

Specialists **escalate to counsel** when foreclosure complaint or sale date is imminent.

## Step-by-step

1. Ask: any foreclosure mail, NOD, or attorney letter? Upload all.
2. Screenshot mortgage tradeline + any public record if on report.
3. FCRA dispute if reporting says “foreclosure completed” but sale has not occurred—exhibit-backed only.
4. Coach partner to use **servicer loss mitigation** in writing—Finely does not file answers.

## Example — late payment vs foreclosure tradeline

Report status suddenly “foreclosure.” Partner only missed one payment and has no court papers. Specialist requests servicer statements; disputes **inaccurate status** if statements show current modification—not “erase mortgage.”
