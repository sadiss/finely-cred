# Score literacy footer (Finely sales packs)

Copy block for emails, SMS, and one-sheets:

---

**Score literacy:** A number without **model + bureau + purpose + soft/hard inquiry** context is not a promise. Free apps often show **VantageScore 3.0**; many mortgage paths use classic **FICO** generations (e.g. Equifax Beacon **5.0**, Experian **FICO 2**, TransUnion **FICO 4**) with a **middle score** rule — underwriting varies. **FICO 8 / 9 / 10 / 10T** adoption varies by lender. Never cite **“FICO 5.8”** as a real model (common 5+8 confusion). Do not assert **“FICO 5.4”** as official — teach **Beacon 5.0** on Equifax. **UltraFICO** is opt-in, not default.

**Finely promise boundary:** Educational credit restore and documentation — **no** guaranteed score increases, deletions, loan approvals, or card approvals. Debt may remain. Nora / lender decisions are separate.
