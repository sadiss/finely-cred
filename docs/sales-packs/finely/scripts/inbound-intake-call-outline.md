# Finely Cred — inbound intake call outline (partner desk)

**Brand:** Finely gold medallion only on handouts — no Nora logos on Finely-primary cold art.

## Open (30 seconds)

- Confirm name, timezone, and whether they uploaded a report yet.
- Set frame: *“We do credit restore and file accuracy — educational, not legal advice. No score guarantees.”*

## Discover (2–3 minutes)

1. What triggered the call? (denial, collections, partner referral, Haitian/Kreyòl kit, Start Restore $147)
2. Debt/collections active? Summons or repo flags? (SOP debt-first)
3. Goal: personal restore, business EIN, tradelines education, or funding-readiness later

## Offer lane

| Signal | Next step |
|--------|-----------|
| Wants fast roadmap + call | **Start Restore $147** → `/start` |
| Full program | Core restore packages → `/services/personal-credit-restore` |
| Partner/agent | Affiliate desk → `/affiliate` |
| Kreyòl preference | `/free-kreyol-guide` or `/haitian` |

## Close

- Book enlightenment session or onboarding link — **staff sends manually** (HQ never auto-sends).
- Nora / lender stacking only after SOP-15 gates — separate conversation, separate brand.

*Educational only. Not legal advice.*
