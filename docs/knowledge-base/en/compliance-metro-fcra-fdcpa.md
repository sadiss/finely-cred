# Compliance literacy — Metro 2, FCRA, FDCPA, CFPB

## Metro 2

Industry reporting format; errors appear as wrong status, dates, or payment grids on the human-readable report. Disputes cite **what the report shows** plus exhibits.

## FCRA

Right to dispute inaccurate or unverifiable information; request method of verification after inadequate responses.

## FDCPA

Collection contact rules; validation; harassment documentation—not shouting matches.

## CFPB

Structured complaint after documented rounds—not day-one emotional spam.

## No guarantees

Educational only; outcomes vary; debt may remain.
