# Credit score intelligence — retrieval summary

Use for portal/academy chat when partners ask about FICO, Vantage, mortgage scores, or why apps disagree with lenders.

## Talk-track (always)

Model + bureau + purpose + soft/hard. No naked numbers. No approval guarantees.

## FICO consumer generations

FICO 8 (cards/common), FICO 9 (paid collections/medical nuances — adoption varies), FICO 10 / **10T** (trended data — adoption not universal).

## Mortgage legacy

Equifax **Beacon 5.0**, Experian **FICO 2**, TransUnion **FICO 4**; **middle/median** score common in education. “FICO 5.4” = unverified jargon — teach Beacon 5.0. “FICO 5.8” = not real.

## Vantage

3.0 on many free apps; 4.0 on some GSE paths — neither equals FICO for all products.

## Industry + UltraFICO

FICO Auto, FICO Bankcard for purpose-specific pulls. UltraFICO = opt-in deposit behavior — optional.

## Finely boundary

Restore and documentation — not lender underwriting. Nora funding is separate.
