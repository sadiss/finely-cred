# Debt validation & summons literacy

## FDCPA-shaped tools (educational)

Consumers may **request validation in writing** within the dispute window after initial collector contact. Finely logs sends in Debt Center; specialists do not litigate for partners.

## Collectors vs original creditors

Not every account is FDCPA-identical; teach process and documentation. Debt buyers must prove chain and amounts—validation starts the paper trail.

## Summons

Never ignore court mail. Calendar answer deadlines; upload to Vault; escalate to counsel. Bureau restore may wait when litigation clocks run.

## Haitian desk note

Explain rights in Kreyòl; bureau/collector letters remain English unless translating concepts for understanding.
