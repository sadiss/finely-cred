# Round 1 — Pages, UX & Funnel Audit

**Scope:** Public site, lead-magnet funnels, and partner-facing (portal/business) pages, immediately following the 14-phase "Deep Marketing & Proof Intelligence Sprint" (case studies, statutory citations, non-citizen/international doctrine, debt/litigation doctrine, business credit doctrine, agent psychology architecture, media production knowledge engine, RAG index, growth-agent upgrades, 5 new UI surfaces).

**Method:** Read `src/App.tsx` route table, `src/config/pricingCatalog.ts`, `src/domain/funnelExperiments.ts` + `src/data/funnelExperimentsRepo.ts`, `src/config/siteWayfinderLanes.ts` (public nav), `src/config/adminNavLanes.ts`, `src/config/publicMediaPolicy.ts`, the new Phase 1–13 surfaces (`TestimonialsPage.tsx`, `PartnerDebtPage.tsx`, `BusinessVendorsPage.tsx`, `BusinessProfilePage.tsx`, `ContentStudioDepartmentPage.tsx`), `FaqPage.tsx`, `ResourcesPage.tsx`, the lead-magnet funnel shell, and the RAG index (`finelyKnowledgeIndex.ts`, `finelyPublicAnswer.ts`). Cross-checked with two 2026 industry sources on credit-repair conversion/trust-signal best practices (see citations at bottom). No application code was changed — this is a planning document only.

## 1. What's genuinely working

- **Real case studies are live and wired into the RAG brain, not just a claim.** `src/data/caseStudiesRepo.ts` feeds both the homepage `ProvenResultsStrip` (`src/components/landing/index.tsx:1931`) and a full filterable case-studies tab inside `/testimonials` (`src/pages/TestimonialsPage.tsx:311-374`), with challenge/strategy/outcome/statutory-basis detail and a compliance line (`Results vary · not legal advice · funding subject to underwriting`). This is exactly the "specific, verifiable outcomes" pattern that converts in this vertical, and it's genuinely built, not vaporware.
- **A/B testing infrastructure is real and already wired into every lead-magnet funnel**, not dormant. `assignFunnelVariant` / `getExperimentForFunnel` / `recordFunnelConversion` (`src/data/funnelExperimentsRepo.ts`) are called directly inside `LeadMagnetFunnelShell.tsx` (`src/components/leadmagnet/LeadMagnetFunnelShell.tsx:21,93,103-106`), which every `/free-*-guide` funnel renders through. Headline/CTA overrides and impression/conversion counters work today.
- **The RAG knowledge index genuinely absorbed the sprint's new doctrine content.** `src/lib/finelyKnowledgeIndex.ts` imports and chunks `getAllCaseStudies`, `getAllDebtLitigationPlaybooks`, `getAllTierStrategies`/`getAllFundingInstruments`, `getAllNonCitizenFundingRules`/`getAllInternationalCreditSystems`, and `getAllAuthorityCitations`. `src/lib/finelyBrain/finelyPublicAnswer.ts` consumes this via `searchFinelyKnowledgePublic` and actively **sanitizes** internal routes (`INTERNAL_ROUTE_PATTERN`, `sanitizeFinelyPublicReply`) so a guest chatting with "Morgan"/"Riley" can be told about debt-litigation phases or non-citizen funding rules without leaking `/portal/debt` or `/business/vendors` URLs. That's careful, deliberate engineering.
- **Nine distinct top-of-funnel lead magnets exist and are genuinely differentiated**, not just one generic ebook: `/free-guide`, `/free-debt-guide`, `/free-business-guide`, `/free-tradeline-guide`, `/free-score-roadmap`, `/free-agency-guide`, `/credit-specialist-guide`, `/real-estate-guide`, `/case-desk-guide`, `/affiliate-toolkit` (`src/App.tsx:2777-2815`). Each has its own landing + reader page pair. This is unusually thorough top-of-funnel coverage for a company this size.
- **The public nav is a genuinely well-organized wayfinder, not just a flat list.** `src/config/siteWayfinderLanes.ts` groups Solutions into "Core paths" vs. "More solutions" and Resources into Guides / Sheet kits / Bookstore / Tools / Community — this is a deliberate progressive-disclosure pattern, and the `SITE_WAYFINDER_LANES` four-lane sticky chooser (personal / business / debt / start-here) gives a clean persona fork.
- **Compliance discipline is consistent.** The `Results vary · not legal advice · funding subject to underwriting` line and `FinelyOsComplianceStrip` recur across `PricingPage.tsx`, `TestimonialsPage.tsx`, and the homepage — this is the CROA/FTC-safe pattern (no guarantee language, disclaimer near proof) that 2026 sources flag as mandatory and is already the house style.
- **Business Credit's 4-tier ladder (Foundation → Builder → Elite → Empire, `src/config/pricingCatalog.ts:640-800`) is a genuinely good pricing model** — clean names, escalating scope, `businessCapitalOutlook` transparently showing program fee + estimated outlay + potential capital range per tier. This is a template the other categories should be pulled toward (see §4).
- **The free-tier entry point on `/pricing` (`personal_free`, $0, DIY, no card) is a strong, low-friction first conversion step** exactly matching the "free consultation/free tool over paid signup" best practice, and it's given its own ivory band treatment at the top of the pricing page (`src/pages/PricingPage.tsx:239-306`) rather than buried in the tier grid.

## 2. What's not working / friction points

- **The single biggest miss: the sprint's flagship new content is invisible to the public and to Google.** The "extreme debt/litigation doctrine" playbook explorer lives at `/portal/debt` (`src/pages/portal/PartnerDebtPage.tsx:22-113`, behind `ProtectedRoute`), the business-credit tier-strategy doctrine lives at `/business/vendors` (`src/pages/business/BusinessVendorsPage.tsx:45-54,318-321`, also `ProtectedRoute`), and the non-citizen/international credit rules live at `/business/profile` (`src/pages/business/BusinessProfilePage.tsx:41-108`, also `ProtectedRoute`, confirmed at `src/App.tsx:1953-1975`). All three require login. None of this proprietary, differentiated, statute-cited content exists as a crawlable public page. It can only be *talked about* by the guest chat persona (which then has to strip the route it would normally cite). A visitor Googling "how to answer a debt collection summons" or "ITIN business credit" will never land on Finely Cred's version of this content — competitors' public blog posts will win that search.
- **Case studies are a tab, not a destination — and not the default tab.** `/testimonials` defaults to `tab === 'videos'` (`src/pages/TestimonialsPage.tsx:164`); a visitor has to click "Case studies" to see the new content, and there is no direct nav entry for it. `src/config/siteWayfinderLanes.ts` Community section only lists "Partner stories" → `/testimonials` (line 279) — no "Case studies" or "Results" link exists anywhere in `PUBLIC_SOLUTIONS_SECTIONS` or `PUBLIC_RESOURCES_SECTIONS`.
- **Proof appears too late in the homepage scroll.** The `ProvenResultsStrip` (case studies) sits in section 10 of ~12 on the homepage (`src/App.tsx:513-567`, comment block `{/* 10. Social proof + compliance */}`), after hero, path chooser, video stage, solutions snapshot, debt-eradication band, AU program, financing, "Mastery OS," and the free-guide teaser. 2026 CRO guidance for financial/credit services is explicit that trust signals should appear **within the first 3 seconds / above the fold** and be repeated throughout the page, not concentrated near the bottom.
- **The homepage's primary hero CTA routes every visitor into Business Credit pricing**, regardless of intent: `<HeroSection onGetStarted={() => navigate('/pricing/business-credit')} ...>` (`src/App.tsx:447`). Personal credit repair is almost certainly the higher-volume, higher-intent search category (per external research, "credit repair" alone gets ~165k searches/month), yet the single highest-leverage click on the site defaults to the business funnel. This may be intentional (higher AOV), but it's worth an explicit test given the A/B infra already exists and is unused for this exact decision.
- **Personal Credit pricing has 10 distinct DFY restore/build tiers with a non-obvious naming ladder**: Starter → Pro (Most Popular) → Elite → Supreme → Apex → Dynasty for restore ($750→$10,000), plus a separate Starter/Pro/Elite build ladder (`src/config/pricingCatalog.ts:306-521`). There is no consistent mental model a first-time visitor can use to map "Supreme" vs. "Apex" to a dollar amount or use case (unlike Business Credit's Foundation/Builder/Elite/Empire, which reads as an obvious progression). Debt & Legal has an even longer 8-tier ladder (`debt_kill_diy` → `..._enterprise`, $297→$39,900, `pricingCatalog.ts:806-1042`). This is choice paralysis on the page category most first-time visitors will land on.
- **A/B testing infra has essentially one live experiment.** `ensureDefaultExperiments()` (`src/data/funnelExperimentsRepo.ts:81-102`) seeds exactly one experiment (`credit_dispute` headline test) and nothing else. All 9 other lead-magnet funnels run through the same `assignFunnelVariant` machinery but have zero configured experiments — the infrastructure is real, but "used everywhere, configured almost nowhere."
- **Public tour/demo videos are hard-disabled site-wide.** `src/config/publicMediaPolicy.ts:5` — `export const PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEOS_ENABLED = false;` — with the comment "admin-only until polished for launch." Combined with the sprint's new "extreme media production knowledge engine" living entirely in admin (`src/features/studioCommandOs/ContentStudioDepartmentPage.tsx`, `/admin/content-studio`), the platform now has serious in-house video *production* capability and zero live public-facing *product* video. Only customer testimonial videos (when published) are visible on `/testimonials`.
- **No competitor-comparison content exists anywhere in `src/pages/`.** A targeted search for "competitor," "vs.," "compare us" in public pages returned nothing relevant. 2026 CRO research specifically flags comparison framing ("inviting the comparison is itself a confidence signal") as a top-of-page-scoring element for finance landing pages — Finely Cred has none.
- **Zero local/city SEO landing pages.** No `src/pages/**/City*.tsx`, no programmatic SEO generator, no `*SeoLanding*` files exist. Credit-repair and debt-defense laws are genuinely state-specific (the new debt-litigation doctrine even distinguishes wage-garnishment exemptions by jurisdiction), which is a strong, defensible reason to build state-level pages — and external research explicitly calls out state-specific landing pages (registration/bonding/compliance shown per state) as a differentiator competitors use.
- **FAQ has zero coverage of the sprint's new categories.** `src/pages/FaqPage.tsx` has 18 items, all about personal credit disputes, ChexSystems, billing, and basic business-credit definitions. Nothing about debt validation/summons/litigation defense (despite an entire `debt_legal` pricing category and now a full litigation-doctrine repo), nothing about non-citizen/ITIN/international funding, nothing about the Wealth Builder ladder.

## 3. What's missing that should exist

- **Public "Knowledge Center" / statute-cited article pages built from the doctrine repos**, e.g. `/resources/debt-defense/summons-answer`, `/resources/business-credit/tier-1-vendors`, `/resources/non-citizen-credit/itin-business-loans` — same underlying data as `debtLitigationDoctrineRepo.ts`, `businessCreditDoctrineRepo.ts`, and `internationalAndNonCitizenCreditRepo.ts`, rendered as crawlable, SEO-metadata'd pages (reusing `usePublicSeoMeta`, same pattern as `BusinessCreditOneSheetsPage.tsx`). This turns three sprint phases' worth of proprietary content into actual organic-search assets instead of gated-only tooling.
- **A dedicated `/results` or `/case-studies` public page** (not just a tab buried in Testimonials) that leads with the strongest case studies, links out into category-specific pricing, and is added as a first-class Solutions/Resources nav entry.
- **Objection-handling / "vs." content**: "Finely Cred vs. DIY," "Finely Cred vs. traditional credit repair," a plain-English page explaining what CROA/FCRA actually require competitors to disclose (turns compliance into a trust weapon, per the DASH-SEO research above).
- **State-level debt-defense landing pages** for the highest-volume states (the doctrine repo already models jurisdiction-sensitive remedies like wage-garnishment exemptions — this is a natural, defensible SEO play that direct competitors in this niche are already doing).
- **A public-facing "before/after" visual proof surface.** The sprint built a before/after score graphic tool (`BeforeAfterScoreGraphicPanel.tsx`), but it lives only in `ContentStudioDepartmentPage.tsx` (admin, `/admin/content-studio`) as a production tool for staff to *generate* graphics — there's no public gallery page that *displays* the finished graphics next to case studies.
- **FAQ sections for Debt & Legal, Wealth Builder, and Non-Citizen/International** — direct, compliant answers to "What happens if I get served a summons?", "Can undocumented/ITIN holders build business credit?", "What's the realistic funding timeline?" would both convert search traffic and pre-handle the objections your growth agents are presumably already answering by hand.
- **A single, explicit "next step" CTA pattern audit-tagged into the design system.** Right now different pages route their financing/upgrade CTA slightly differently (`resolveFinelyCtaPath`, `finelyCtaNavigate`, direct `navigate('/pricing/...')`, `handleSelect`, `startFinancingPreapprovalInterest`) — functionally fine, but there's no single documented "this is the site's canonical next-step CTA" contract that new pages are required to reuse, which is how the hero's business-credit default (§2) went unnoticed.
- **Trust-badge strip near the top of `/pricing` and the homepage** (BBB-style accreditation if/when eligible, years-in-business, aggregate "X disputes filed" or "$X funding secured" counters using real `caseStudiesRepo` aggregates) — `PricingPage.tsx` currently opens straight into the `PricingSolutionsHero` + free-plan sell with no trust strip above the tab bar.
- **A short, compliant explainer video embedded on the homepage and `/pricing`** once `PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEOS_ENABLED` is safe to flip — even a 60–90s "how disputes work" video would leverage the newly-built media production knowledge engine for something visitors can actually see today.

## 4. What exists but should be removed/simplified

- **Collapse the Personal Credit Restore ladder from 6 named DFY tiers to 3–4.** Starter/Pro/Supreme/Apex/Dynasty ($750/$1,500/$3,000/$5,000/$7,000/$10,000) is more granularity than a first-time visitor can reason about. Business Credit's 4-tier Foundation/Builder/Elite/Empire naming is the better mental model — collapse Supreme/Apex/Dynasty into a single "Elite+" custom-quote tier the way Agency Enterprise already does (`pricingCatalog.ts:1868-1892`, `pricingModel: 'custom'`).
- **Debt & Legal's 8-tier ladder is redundant with the debt-balance guidance table already on the page.** `getDebtPackageGuidanceForBalance()` (`pricingCatalog.ts:2119-2130`) already auto-recommends a tier from balance — the pricing page could show 3–4 headline tiers (Starter / Pro / High-Balance / Enterprise-by-quote) and let the balance-guidance table do the fine-grained routing, instead of showing all 8 SKUs.
- **`chexsystems_cleanup` / `early_warning_cleanup` living inside `personalCreditPackages` but requiring a separate "Banking Reports" tab with manual filtering logic** (`PricingPage.tsx:177-180`, `getPackagesByCategory` filtered out at line 164) is unnecessary indirection — either give Banking Reports its own top-level `PricingCategory`, or fold it as an add-on chip inside the Personal tab rather than a 9th top-level pricing tab most visitors won't think to click.
- **`FunnelExperiment` scaffolding with only one seeded experiment is dead weight if left unused much longer** — either populate 2–3 more experiments (headline tests on `/free-debt-guide` and `/free-business-guide` would be high-value given their pricing tiers) or explicitly deprioritize the admin `AdminFunnelExperimentsPage.tsx` surface so it doesn't look like a finished feature that owners think is "already running."
- **Multiple legacy/duplicate redirect routes for the same destination** add route-table noise without user-facing harm but complicate future audits: `/fix-my-credit`, `/build-my-credit`, `/debt-summons-help`, `/business-credit-solutions`, `/business-credit` all `Navigate` to canonical `/pricing/...` paths (`src/App.tsx:1479-1484`); `/guides`, `/one-sheets`, `/partner-stories`, `/blog`, `/diy-academy`, `/rent-reporting` similarly redirect (`1486-1507`). Not urgent to remove (likely preserved for old ad/backlink traffic) but worth a periodic audit — some of these six+ redirect chains suggest historical naming churn that should stop, not continue.

## 5. Content delivery mechanics

- **Pacing:** The homepage is a long single-scroll (~12 sections: hero → path chooser → cinematic video stage → solutions snapshot → debt-eradication band → AU program → financing → "Mastery OS" → free-guide teaser → social proof/compliance → final CTA → footer). That's a reasonable amount of content, but the *order* front-loads product/mechanism sections (video stage, Mastery OS device cluster) before trust proof, which fights against the "reassure early and often" pattern financial-services CRO research recommends. Consider moving a condensed proof strip (3 case-study cards) to directly follow the hero, with the full `ProvenResultsStrip` staying where it is for depth.
- **Text vs. video:** The site is overwhelmingly text/UI-driven. The only video content reliably reaching a public visitor is user-submitted testimonial video (`/testimonials`, gated on whatever's been recorded and published) — there is no scripted, produced explainer/demo content live (`PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEOS_ENABLED = false`). For a credit-repair audience that skews toward wanting reassurance from a human face before committing money, this is a real gap given the platform now has a dedicated media production knowledge engine (`ContentStudioDepartmentPage.tsx`) that isn't yet producing anything the public can watch.
- **Reading format for deep content:** The dense new doctrine (debt litigation phases, business-credit tier strategies, non-citizen funding rules) is delivered as chip-driven interactive explorers (`DebtDefensePlaybookExplorer` in `PartnerDebtPage.tsx`, tier-strategy cards in `BusinessVendorsPage.tsx`) rather than long-form prose — that's a genuinely good, scannable UX pattern *once a user is logged in*, but because it's portal-only, that good pattern never reaches a cold visitor who'd need long-form public prose (with schema markup) to be found by search at all. The formats aren't wrong; the audience gating is.
- **Mobile:** Spot-checked responsive classes are present throughout (`sm:`/`lg:` breakpoints on hero, pricing tabs use a horizontal-scroll pattern at `PricingPage.tsx:387-402` — `-mx-1 px-1 overflow-x-auto` for the 9-tab strip). That horizontal-scroll tab bar is a reasonable mobile pattern for 9 tabs, but 9 tabs is still a lot to horizontally scroll through on a phone before finding, e.g., Debt & Legal — reinforces the case for collapsing tabs (§4) independent of desktop concerns. No obvious click-to-call CTA pattern was found in the pages reviewed, which external research flags as a materially better mobile conversion mechanism than a form for anxious, ready-to-act credit-distress visitors.
- **Compliance copy placement:** Consistently done as a strip/footnote near proof and pricing (`FinelyOsComplianceStrip`, `FINELY_OS_COMPLIANCE_FOOTNOTE`) rather than buried in a terms page — this matches the "keep answering doubt everywhere" principle from the research and should be the template extended to any new public doctrine pages built from §3.

## 6. Prioritized recommendations

1. **Publish 8–12 public, SEO-indexable articles from the debt-litigation, business-credit-tier, and non-citizen doctrine repos** (new `/learn/...` or `/resources/...` routes reusing `usePublicSeoMeta`) — this is the single highest-leverage move because the content already exists and is currently invisible to Google and cold visitors. *Effort: M.*
2. **Add a top-level "Case Studies" / "Results" nav entry and dedicated `/results` page**, pulling from `caseStudiesRepo.ts`, rather than leaving it as a non-default tab inside `/testimonials`. *Rationale: proof this strong should not require a click-to-discover.* *Effort: S.*
3. **Move a condensed proof/trust strip to directly beneath the homepage hero**, keeping the full `ProvenResultsStrip` further down for depth-readers. *Rationale: trust signals convert better in the first 3 seconds than at scroll position 10 of 12.* *Effort: S.*
4. **A/B test the homepage hero CTA destination (`/pricing/business-credit` vs. `/pricing/personal-credit-restore` vs. `/start-here`)** using the already-built `funnelExperimentsRepo` infra. *Rationale: the single highest-traffic click on the site currently defaults to one persona without evidence it's the right one.* *Effort: S.*
5. **Collapse Personal Credit Restore (6 tiers) and Debt & Legal (8 tiers) into 3–4 headline tiers + a "talk to us" custom tier**, mirroring the cleaner Business Credit ladder. *Rationale: choice paralysis on the two categories most first-time visitors browse.* *Effort: M.*
6. **Build a public "before/after" proof gallery page using the new score-graphic tool's output**, so a tool currently only producing assets for staff (`BeforeAfterScoreGraphicPanel.tsx`) actually reaches visitors. *Effort: M.*
7. **Add FAQ coverage for Debt & Legal (summons/validation), Non-Citizen/International, and Wealth Builder** — direct extensions of `FaqPage.tsx`'s existing pattern, using the new doctrine repos as source material. *Effort: S.*
8. **Populate 2–3 more funnel experiments** (headline/CTA tests on `/free-debt-guide` and `/free-business-guide`, which carry the highest-ticket downstream pricing) instead of leaving `credit_dispute` as the only configured test. *Effort: S.*
9. **Build 3–5 state-specific debt-defense landing pages** for top-volume states, leveraging the jurisdiction-aware remedies already modeled in `debtLitigationDoctrineRepo.ts`. *Rationale: defensible, compliance-forward local SEO competitors in this niche already use.* *Effort: L.*
10. **Add one short, compliant explainer/demo video to the homepage and `/pricing`**, and only then reconsider flipping `PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEOS_ENABLED`. *Rationale: the platform now has real video production capability with zero public output.* *Effort: M.*
11. **Add a compliance-forward "vs. DIY / vs. traditional credit repair" comparison page.** *Rationale: comparison framing reads as a confidence signal per 2026 CRO research, and Finely Cred currently has none.* *Effort: M.*
12. **Document one canonical "next step" CTA contract** (which helper — `resolveFinelyCtaPath`, `finelyCtaNavigate`, or a new single wrapper — every new public page must use) so CTA-destination decisions like #4 are made deliberately, not by whichever helper a page happened to import. *Effort: S.*
