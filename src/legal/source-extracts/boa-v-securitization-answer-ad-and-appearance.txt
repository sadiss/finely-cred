In violation of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (“FDCPA") and Regulation of Michigan Collection Practices Act ("RMCPA), Plaintiff is suing Mr. Dorothy Debtor for a possible Breach of Contract with no cardholder agreement between Bank of America, N.A. (BOA) attached to the lawsuit. Further, Defendant does not owe Plaintiff as the chain of title in this lawsuit lacks any evidentiary proof of the SPECIFIC DEBT of Mr. Debtor being assigned from either a secured trust or FIA Card Services, N.A. under Michigan law and the Brownbark, Bassett and Riley cases below.
