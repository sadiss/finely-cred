PRELIMINARY STATEMENT AS TO NO STATED CLAIM
In violation of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (“FDCPA”) and Regulation of Michigan Collection Practices Act (“RMCPA), Plaintiff is suing Mr.                     for an ACCOUNT STATED with no agreement between Citibank, NA (“Citibank”) and with no payment made on the debt by Defendant to the debt collector, Stenger & Stenger, P.C. for there to be Account Stated Claim under Michigan law. 
The debt is not owned by Citibank and is located in a secured trust. See the Citibank/Citibank 10K Analysis on the securitization of its credit card receivables at Exhibit 2.  See also Exhibit 3 Explanation of Securitization. 
Further the words, “This is a communication from a debt collector. We are attempting to collect a debt and any information obtained will be used for that purpose” was written on the Summons and Complaint for the process server and third party to show Mr.                   was being pursued by a debt collector:

 
This is a violation of 15 USC 1692e (11) and the FDCPA prohibition of placing the “mini-Miranda” warning on lawsuits or pleadings below:
(11) The failure to disclose in the initial written communication with the consumer and, in addition, if the initial communication with the consumer is oral, in that initial oral communication, that the debt collector is attempting to collect a debt and that any information obtained will be used for that purpose, and the failure to disclose in subsequent communications that the communication is from a debt collector, except that this paragraph shall not apply to a formal pleading made in connection with a legal action.

As Plaintiff has no standing to sue Defendant with no ownership proof or assignment from the secured trust was placed in at origination to sue                , this case should be dismissed. Thank you.
