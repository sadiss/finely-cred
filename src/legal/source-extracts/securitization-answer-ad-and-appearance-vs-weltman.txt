Creditors are not regulated by the FDCPA. Here Discover’s status as a debt collector on a defaulted debt under the FDCPA is confirmed in that there is no Assignment showing the debt or receivables were returned to Discover Bank from the Master Trust or Discovery Funding, LLC for it to be collecting for itself. The Appearance of Discover Products shows there are other entities servicing the debt for a Master Trust or ABS. 
With no agreement with Plaintiff or its law firm or no payment made by Defendant, there cannot be an Account Stated Claim against Defendant under Michigan law. There is no contract attached to the lawsuit or pleadings. Please see Exhibit 1. Further, Defendant does not owe Plaintiff as the chain of title in this lawsuit lacks any evidentiary proof of the SPECIFIC DEBT of  Ms. Debtor being assigned back to Plaintiff from the ABS under Michigan law. See Exhibit 1 Affidavit and Securitization Information and the Complaint and Exhibit 2, Trust Documentation of Discovery Bank/Discover Holding, LLC.
With no proof of ownership of this debt, Plaintiff has no standing to sue Ms. Debtorand no jurisdiction over Defendant, this case should be dismissed. Thank you.
