# Video Scene Description / Thumbnail Prompt

Use this for the video thumbnail shown in the landing page video section.

## Scene

A luxury fintech conference room / boardroom at dusk, designed for a high-end credit strategy brand.

## Visual composition

- Wide cinematic shot.
- Long dark executive conference table centered in the room.
- Polished black or deep walnut tabletop with subtle reflections.
- Premium leather executive chairs lined along both sides of the table.
- Floor-to-ceiling windows behind the table.
- City skyline visible through the windows, slightly out of focus.
- Golden hour / dusk lighting outside, not bright daytime.
- Warm gold light reflections across the table edges.
- Deep emerald and charcoal color grading.
- Subtle gold accents, not flashy.
- No people visible.
- No clutter.
- No logos.
- No text on the video image.
- No diamonds.
- No rainbow effects.
- No home/interior design styling; it should feel like private-client finance, institutional strategy, and executive credit consulting.

## Camera / mood

- Professional commercial video thumbnail.
- High contrast, cinematic, luxury, quiet power.
- Slight vignette around the corners.
- Strong focal point in the center where the play button will appear.
- 16:9 aspect ratio.

## Negative prompt

Avoid: living room, home office, marble showroom, generic office stock photo, bright corporate blue office, cartoon render, diamonds, neon rainbow, messy desk, people, visible brand logos, text overlays.
